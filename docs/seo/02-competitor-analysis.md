# 02 · Competitors and market (first pass, 25 Nov 2026)

**Method:** web searches for "jewellery recruitment agency India" and "jewellery CAD designer hiring consultant Mumbai Surat", plus the two competitors named in the brief. No paid SEO tool (Ahrefs, Semrush, DataForSEO) was used, so traffic, keyword volume and backlink data are **unknown**. Only what is visible in search results is recorded here.

## What the results tell us
- **The employer queries have little competition.** "Jewellery recruitment agency India" returns general recruiters and thin city pages, not a jewellery-only recruiter with depth. That is the opening.
- **Candidate queries are owned by job boards.** "Jewellery CAD designer jobs Mumbai" is Indeed's ground. Our job pages can still show in **Google for Jobs** once they are indexable (fixed: they were `noindex`).
- **Nobody publishes useful employer resources** (role skill checklists, what to ask a CAD candidate, staffing a new showroom). These are the citation-worthy assets AI answers pick up.

## Differentiation (only what is true today)
- Jewellery-only, across retail, manufacturing, wholesale, export, diamonds and D2C.
- Screens before sharing: every candidate is called and interviewed.
- Pay on joining; free replacement window (`replacement_days` in settings, 90).
- Live, real openings on the site, linked from the service pages.
- WhatsApp-first process and a client account.
