Super admin — 1-page guide

BrillBrains head office. You see and control everything.

Every morning

  • Open Dashboard: work down Needs attention — each line has Open and a green WhatsApp button.
  • Open Leads: assign each new client and candidate to an agency (the agency admin gets a task alert).
  • Check WhatsApp for unread replies (number in the menu).

Agencies and logins

Logins & team → Add agency (with its admin) or Add login. The password shows once and goes once on WhatsApp. Reset password the same way. Pause an agency or turn a login off — records stay, they just cannot log in. Locked after 5 wrong passwords? Press Unlock.

Money

Bills: a draft is made when someone is marked Joined. Check it, then Issue. Record each payment (part payments and TDS allowed). When fully paid, the agency payout becomes Due. Tick due payouts and Mark selected payouts paid with the bank reference. Money shows billed, received, overdue and shares by week and month.

Watching the teams

Performance: agencies table (On track / Needs attention / Inactive); click an agency to see its people. Daily logs: approve the admins’ logs. Reports: funnel, per client, per job, sources, money, replacements — each downloads to Excel.

Settings (only you)

  • Lists (jobs, cities, skills, stages, reasons, sources), fees, GST, targets, limits.
  • Message formats: send to Meta for approval, then Check approval status.
  • Automatic messages: turn each rule on or off.
  • A change applies to new records only.

Health and privacy

  • Settings → Health: last WhatsApp sent / reply / timer run, failed messages (Retry), backups, test restore.
  • Settings → Data requests: approve “delete my data” requests.
  • Before go-live: Settings → Business → Remove sample data.
