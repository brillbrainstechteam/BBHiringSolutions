Recruiter (sub admin) — 1-page guide

You work on the jobs where you are the recruiter.

Start of the day

  • Open My day: today’s interviews, results to record, follow-ups, applications stuck 7+ days.
  • Check WhatsApp for replies from your candidates and clients.

Finding candidates

  • Open a job → Best matches → Add (makes an application at Applied).
  • Candidates: filter the resume bank; tick many → Add to a job.
  • Other agencies’ candidates show without a phone number — the WhatsApp button still works.
  • + Add lead (top bar) for walk-ins and calls; Upload Excel for job-site downloads.

Moving an application

  1. Applied → Screening (press →)
  2. Schedule our interview → after it, Record result (questions, rating, remark). Move ahead sends the profile to the client.
  3. Client taps Interested → Client interview; schedule it.
  4. Client says Selected → Record the offer → Make offer letter.
  5. Candidate accepts → on the joining day Mark joined.

WhatsApp

  • Every green button opens one window: suggested format first, English / Hindi, details filled in, preview.
  • If they messaged in the last 24 hours you can type freely; otherwise only an approved format.
  • 9 pm–9 am messages wait until 9 am.

Daily log

  • Fill Daily logs before 7 pm: tasks done, problems, plan for tomorrow. Your numbers are counted for you.

Remember

  • Red days on a card = 7+ days in one step — move it or put it On hold with a reason.
  • Remind me on any record sets a follow-up.
  • Search anything (ID, name, phone) from the top bar.
