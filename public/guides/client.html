Client account — 1-page guide

For businesses hiring through BrillBrains.

Logging in

  • Open your account link and enter your WhatsApp number. Type the 6-digit code we send on WhatsApp.
  • HR and store managers can have their own login: Company & papers → People from your company.

Raise a job opening

  • Press Raise a job opening: the job, city, how many people, salary per month.
  • We write the job description and send it to you: Approve or Ask for changes under Job openings.
  • Pause hiring any time; on a filled job press I need this role again.

Review candidates

Candidates shows profiles we send you: experience, salary, notice, skills match, our interview notes. Press Interested, set up interview (the phone number then shows) or Not suitable.

Interviews and offers

Interviews: after meeting a candidate, press Give feedback: Selected, Another round or Not selected. When you select someone we record the offer; check the offer letter and press Send to candidate. Tick the joining checklist when they join.

Bills

  • A bill is made when your hire joins: the fee on the yearly salary plus GST. Pay by UPI from the Bills tab.
  • If a hire leaves on their own in the first 3 months, press Ask for free replacement.

Papers and help

Company & papers: upload KYC, sign the contract, edit your offer letter and joining form templates, download monthly summaries. Questions? Press Need help? at the top to WhatsApp BrillBrains.
