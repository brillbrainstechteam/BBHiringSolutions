Admin (agency owner) — 1-page guide

You run one partner agency and see only its work.

Every morning

Dashboard → Needs attention: results not recorded, stuck applications, jobs with no candidates, job descriptions to write, missing logs, overdue bills. The green button sends the right WhatsApp to the right person (for example a nudge to your recruiter).

Clients

  • New leads assigned to you show in Clients (and your bell).
  • Open a client: set the stage, tick onboarding (intro call, proposal, KYC, contract), add notes.
  • + New job opening from the client panel. Tick “JD approved” only if the client already approved it.

Job openings

  • Status is worked out by itself — never typed (except On hold and Closed).
  • Write JD → tick “Send to client” → the client approves in their account.
  • Pick the recruiter; tick posting channels and copy each tagged link.
  • Job alert to top matches sends to strong matches who agreed to alerts.

Your team

Logins & team → Add login for your recruiters (sub admins); reset or turn off. Performance: your sub admins’ numbers, log dots and target %. Daily logs: fill your own log and approve your team’s before the deadline.

Earnings

Bills shows your agency’s bills and the agency share on each. Earnings tab: month-by-month statement — shares paid, due and not yet due. Download it to Excel.

Good habits

  • Record every interview result the same day.
  • Move cards as soon as something happens — performance is counted from these moves.
  • Use the suggested WhatsApp message at the top of each application.
